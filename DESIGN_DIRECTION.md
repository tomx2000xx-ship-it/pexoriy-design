# Pexoriy Design Direction

## Brand
Energetic, community-driven, premium, intelligent, and approachable.

## Visual system
- Primary accent: acid lime `#B7F34A`
- Background: deep charcoal `#10120F`
- Surface: `#171A15`
- Elevated surface: `#20231D`
- Muted text: `#92978A`
- Optional AI accent: lavender `#B9A3FF`
- Negative state: `#FF8585`

## Product principles
- Keep layouts simple, classic, polished, and mobile-first.
- Avoid generic exchange templates, excessive gradients, clutter, and childish gamification.
- Trading is simulated using market data; do not imply real exchange execution or withdrawable simulated P&L.
- Trade Credits and AI Credits are product credits, not cash.
- Keep any future project token separate and avoid inventing tokenomics or guaranteed returns.

## Screens to design
Dashboard, Practice Trading, AI Trading Centre, Competition Hub and detail/leaderboard, Missions, Referrals, Achievements, Activity Centre, future token page, wallet/account, profile/settings, onboarding, and loading/empty/error/success/disabled states.

## Implementation constraints
React JSX with Vite; no TypeScript/TSX and no Next.js. Mobile-first responsive layout with bottom navigation. Preserve the existing repository structure.
